# Source and provenance

Original Patronus guidance authored on 2026-10-14. It is the single maintained
body for the decision-clarity-cl, decision-clarity-cx, decision-clarity-pi and
decision-clarity-oc entrypoints, which read this bundle at its exact installed
path. No upstream text is vendored; see NOTICE and LICENSE (GPL-3.0).

Content is grounded in local comparisons of existing Patronus writing, grilling
and brainstorming skills and in invented before/after decision asks. Those
comparisons support the wording; they do not demonstrate comprehension gains,
token savings or runtime compliance.

The presentation lens (a few short narrative paragraphs rather than a compact
mapping) follows one owner's stated design-time preference. It is a style
choice for this guidance, not evidence that every reader prefers it.

Every example in references/examples.md is invented. Names, paths, counts and
outcomes there are illustrative, not observations. Static placement of this
bundle is not evidence of native loading or behavior.
