# Complete the authorized OpenCode stage

Reread the brief, latest lead decisions, selected progress authority and source
state. Report actual criteria against exact root/base/head and relevant dirty,
untracked and ignored input hashes. Consume retained evidence rather than creating
a finishing-only suite or review. Reuse checks only when checked bytes, full
command/options, relevant dependency/tool/environment identity and acceptance
scope match, with readable logs and actual exits. HEAD equality alone is not enough;
relevant drift, changed acceptance or missing evidence invalidates reuse. Message
or stage changes alone do not. Final aggregate verification stays fresh for the
composed delivered inputs; matching integration checks can satisfy finishing.
Include changed files, tests added, commands/exits/logs, input/output hashes,
skipped or blocked checks, review dispositions, residual risks, open asks and
next authorized action. Preserve durable partial evidence when a lane fails.

Settle every dependent worker before transition or cleanup. Read complete final
responses and checked patches; a dispatch receipt or success sentence is not
acceptance. Unknown liveness/ownership or failed capture retains allocations for
the lead. Read-only workers return findings; the authorized lead writes shared
outputs and progress records. No OpenCode mission API is invented or required.

Consume the required independent aggregate implementation review. One matching
review satisfies feature/branch/pre-merge triggers for the same exact inputs,
scope and coverage; finishing adds no new whole-change review. Only the coordinator
admits reviewers or at most one bounded batched correction/disposition per gate.
Leaves return complete findings and stop, without successors or shared status writes.
Unresolved Critical/Major or more than two distinct Medium block; up to two Medium require
explicit owner-accepted residuals. Missing authority, requirements, tests or
acceptance independently block. Report corrected bytes for lead disposition;
never auto-launch a second review wave or silently change model/provider/protocol.
The coordinator verifies a fixed defect against its cited acceptance criterion,
exact corrected bytes and relevant positive/negative/regression evidence. Retain
that disposition as parent-verified, not independently re-reviewed. A changed
public interface, safety invariant or acceptance scope, or an insufficient oracle,
requires an owner decision or additional independent-review grant. Critical/Major
residuals cannot be waived, and severity cannot be lowered to fit the threshold.

A checked local handoff completes only its authorized stage. If publication is
ungranted, local evidence delivery is the completion boundary, not an obligation
to obtain push authority. Existing valid grants continue within scope; a new
stage label does not require repeated consent. Commit, integration, publication
and cleanup remain separate grants. There is no automatic push, pull,
rebase, stash deletion, branch/worktree removal or prune. Capture complete patches
and reports before any authorized cleanup; retain work when capture or settlement
is uncertain. Do not claim remote publication or native runtime readiness from
local fixture tests or static placement.
