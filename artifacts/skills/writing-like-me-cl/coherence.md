# The conceptual coherence audit

This is a fresh, whole-piece audit of ideas and connections. It does not score voice and it does
not edit prose. Receive the source claims and evidence bounds, citations, the approved spine and
definitions, and the current candidate. Do not receive the voice profile, liveness rubric or an
instruction to improve diction, rhythm, register, imagery density or author-likeness.

Write `COHERENCE.md` as the deliverable. Prefer three to seven prioritised findings over exhaustive
commentary. If there are more defects, group instances that share one correction. A pass still
records the candidate hash, materials inspected and the checks performed.

## Finding contract

Each finding must contain:

1. the exact draft span;
2. granularity and failure type;
3. what the reader can actually recover in the proximal window;
4. the conceptual or domain correction, bounded by the sources;
5. the earliest downstream return point; and
6. optionally, one bridge labelled `illustrative only`, never replacement prose.

Do not rewrite the span. Do not choose cadence, metaphor, diction or register. A separate fresh
voice-owning editor decides how to express an accepted correction and accounts for it in
`COHERENCE-RESPONSE.md`.

## Required checks

Audit at all of these granularities:

| Unit | Check |
|---|---|
| phrase | Every demonstrative, pronoun, positional pointer, unnamed move, conversation or threshold has one exact recoverable antecedent in the nearby prose. Similar vocabulary elsewhere is not recovery. |
| sentence | Causal direction, category and ontology are correct; coined abstractions are defined before they carry reasoning; the observer can physically or epistemically know what the sentence claims. |
| paragraph | Concrete material is available at this point in the argument and earns the abstraction; the paragraph develops rather than summarises its conclusion in advance. |
| paragraph boundary | State what the reader carries forward and whether the next paragraph develops it, turns it, or makes an intelligible hard cut. Flag individually sound paragraphs with no conceptual dependency. Do not demand a generic signpost. |
| section boundary | The opening re-establishes inherited pressure or begins a purposeful new movement; it does not preview the section's claims or rely on a cold image. |
| whole piece | The governing model remains internally consistent and faithful to source evidence; balanced prose does not hide category errors or unsupported equivalences. |

For a governing story, myth or metaphor, test narrative situatedness as well as factual accuracy.
A reader unfamiliar with it must receive the scene conditions, relevant character intent and the
reason the image matters. Verify and cite material factual detail. Test geometry and point of view:
an entrant cannot describe an aerial layout unless the prose earns that perspective.

Definition-before-use applies to attractive coinages too. Identify whose property the term is,
what it permits someone to know or do, and establish that meaning proximally before repetition
turns it into working equipment.

Domain nouns must arrive only after the argument makes them available. A specific rule, backend or
deployment object is not automatically a good concrete example if the reader has not yet entered
that domain; use an already available object or first establish the context.

Technical claims are audited for category and causal correctness, not merely source-word survival.
For configuration reconciliation, for example, distinguish desired configuration, observed state,
and the history or outcomes of attempted transitions. A repository recording desired configuration
does not become a record of reality merely because a reconciler compares or acts on reality. Treat
this as an example of the check, not prose to insert or a universal architecture.

## Verdict and routing

`PASS` means no material conceptual or reader-state failure remains. Otherwise return a blocking
finding set. Route a source, evidence or ontology defect to the spine/manifest; a governing model
defect to the spine; an order or placement defect to synthesis; a developmental or opening defect
to journey; and a local join, definition, deictic, perspective or domain correction to the fresh
coherence-response editor. When a correction changes an earlier authority, rerun all dependent
stages rather than patching downstream.
